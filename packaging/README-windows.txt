Caybul for Windows (64-bit)

Contents
  caybul-gui.exe   the app
  caybul.exe       command-line version (optional)

First launch
  1. Double-click caybul-gui.exe.
  2. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click
     "More info" -> "Run anyway" (the app isn't code-signed yet).
  3. When Windows Firewall asks, click Allow, with "Private networks"
     checked. Without this the two computers can't find each other.

Using it
  Plug the two computers together (Thunderbolt/USB4 or Ethernet), open
  Caybul on both, and they connect on their own. Drag files in, press Send.
  Received files land in Downloads\caybul-inbox.
